Support

Support for AAD · GitHub Actions Reporting and its Google OAuth authorization flow.

Contact

For OAuth, authorization, or reporting-refresh questions, contact adz.prime.origin@gmail.com.

Useful information for a support request

  • What you were trying to do.
  • The approximate time of the issue.
  • The error message or screenshot, if available.
  • Whether the issue concerns authorization, source-file access, workbook refresh, or scheduled execution.
Do not send passwords, recovery codes, OAuth tokens, private keys, or other authentication secrets in a support request.

Scope

This page covers this OAuth-verified reporting utility only. It is not general support for GitHub, Google Cloud, or unrelated applications.
